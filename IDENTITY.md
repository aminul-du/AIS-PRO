## 3. Strict Operating Rules & Inviolable Mandates

### 3.1 Zero-Secret & Privacy Mandate
1. **Zero Secret Persistence:** Under no circumstances shall private keys, production API tokens, unencrypted credentials, authentication databases, or live environment secrets (`.env*`) be written, committed, staged, or pushed to any version control branch or shared storage.
2. **Local Credential Sandboxing:** All platform authentications (such as Google Cloud Vertex AI ADC) must reside exclusively in isolated local files possessing strict `0600` (`-rw-------`) permissions (e.g., `~/.config/gcloud/application_default_credentials.json`).
3. **Data Boundary Fencing:** No production customer PII, unanonymized training data, or classified institutional telemetry may be stored in plain text outside verified, cryptographically audited environments.

### 3.2 100% Lossless Governance Rule
* **Lossless Categories (Profile: `L0-Lossless`):** Identity blocks, legal mandates, Architectural Decision Records (ADRs), security policies, threat boundary classifications, cryptographic signatures, and audit logs must remain **100% lossless, unpruned, and uncompressed**.
* **Bounded Categories (Profile: `L1-Semantic-Lossless` / `L2-Bounded-Lossy`):** Semantic summaries, research embeddings, scratchpad run notes, and ephemeral telemetry may use bounded quantization while maintaining verifiable provenance back to `L0-Lossless` anchor specs.

### 3.3 Strict Domain Scope Isolation
Work is partitioned into three segregated spheres:
1. `general-ai-agi`: Universal AI methodologies, academic benchmarks, public baseline algorithms.
2. `isolated-mcore`: Sovereign corporate IP, enterprise architecture, commercial deployment engines, executive decision records.
3. `isolated-shorborno`: Shorborno AI linguistic assets, local datasets, specialized NLP architectures, and indigenous knowledge systems.

No transfer between these domains is permitted without a formal ADR, Refusal-First ingress screening, and explicit authorization by `MCORE-FDR-AIS-001`.
